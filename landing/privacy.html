1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Neuravolt Technologies Private Limited ("Neuravolt", "Company", "we", "us", or "our") processes, collects, stores, and protects personal data obtained from users of our Platform.

2. Information We Collect

We collect data in the following categories:

  • Identity & Profile: Names, corporate login credentials, dates of account creation.
  • Billing Data: GSTIN, billing addresses, invoice transaction records. (Payments are processed securely via Razorpay/Stripe).
  • Prompt & RAG Data: Text prompts, uploaded PDF/DOCX documents, vector index embeddings, chat histories.
  • System Diagnostics: IP addresses, token usage counts, model inference latency metrics.

3. Legal Basis of Processing

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 of India, we process data based on:

  1. Unambiguous Consent provided during registration.
  2. Contractual Necessity to deliver dedicated workspace container nodes.
  3. Compliance with Indian legal directives (CERT-In security reporting rules).

4. Zero AI Training Policy

Strict Guarantee: Neuravolt does NOT use your proprietary prompts, RAG document indexes, or AI outputs to train, fine-tune, or improve public baseline LLM models. All tenant data is cryptographically isolated under dedicated Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.

5. Data Security & Localization

Data Localization: All database volumes, vector indexes, and backup snapshots for Indian accounts reside on secure cloud data centers located within the borders of India.

Encryption Standards: Data is encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM and in transit using TLS 1.3 protocols.

6. Your Data Principal Rights

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of your personal data processed on the platform.
  • Request immediate correction or updating of inaccurate data.
  • Request complete erasure of your workspace databases ("Right to Erasure").
  • Nominate a representative in case of incapacity.
